走著瞧！

Look over There!

2022

年份 Year
2022
媒材 Medium
壓克力顏料於牆面
尺寸／片長 Dimensions / duration
依場地而定

我企圖用脫離架上的現地繪畫去找尋不同以往的作品觀看方式。觀者不只是單純佇足於作品前被動的觀看作品，而是以一種游擊戰的模式，受到引導而主動去探索展場，尋找作品，甚至是轉身一撇，與牆面的圖像相視，眼角餘光裡混淆了作品與現實的差異。我試圖以繪畫圖像為媒介，介入空間與觀眾之間，探索一種移動式的觀看狀態，提升觀者對展覽場域的敏感度。在這系列的嘗試中，我選取展覽場域會出現的人，或是與展場連結性強的人像來製造觀者第一時間的錯認；我也在作品《走著瞧！》中複製展場周邊的咖啡廳標誌，並用模板及噴漆將標誌噴在展場靠近咖啡廳的牆上，讓這個空間乍看似乎成為了咖啡廳的一部份，想讓對空間疑惑或是要進入咖啡聽的觀眾走過來觀看，並透過這個錯誤的引導，模糊美術館展場與咖啡廳的分界。

Read in English

With the intention of departing from traditional exhibition methods, I aim to explore alternative ways of viewing artworks through on-site painting detached from conventional frameworks. The viewer is not merely a passive observer standing before the artwork but, rather, adopts a guerrilla-like approach, actively exploring the exhibition space, searching for the artwork, and perhaps even turning around to glance at the wall images. In the peripheral vision, the distinction between the artwork and reality becomes blurred.

Using painted images as a medium, I seek to intervene in the space between the artwork and the audience, exploring a mobile state of viewing that heightens the viewer's sensitivity to the exhibition space. In this series of experiments, I select figures that would typically be present in the exhibition space or portraits with strong connections to the venue to create initial misconceptions for the viewer. In the piece "Look over there!" I replicate the café logos around the exhibition area and use stencils and spray paint to apply these logos on the walls near the café, creating the illusion that this space is part of the café. I aim to lead viewers, who may be curious about the space or interested in entering the café, to approach and observe. Through this misguided guidance, the boundaries between the art gallery and the café become blurred.
