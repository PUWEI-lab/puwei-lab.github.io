顯視錯誤：404

vision404

共同創作 Collaboration
吳柏葳、黃怡嘉

吳柏葳與黃怡嘉的創作靈感，來自於日常生活中的影像及流行文化圖像觀察。在過去幾次的展覽，他們以敏銳的視角和游擊式的策略，透過噴漆所做的限地作品介入展場空間，藉由扁平的圖層和當代社會影像過剩的狀態對話。

在本展中，吳柏葳與黃怡嘉使用噴漆做為創作的中介，讓作品蔓生於當代館的各個角落。部分作品鑲嵌在觀看的路徑，如同投影的影像、也鬆動觀眾餘光所捕捉的日常感知。展場入口的作品《顯視錯誤：404》，以聲音和影像構成一個撩撥觀眾視聽經驗的裝置，隨著觀展路徑上四散的噴漆影像，藝術家意圖在展場中創造一個索引式佈局，唯有繞行完展場，觀眾才能拼湊出作品的樣貌。在平面影像大量充斥的時代，吳柏葳與黃怡嘉透過影像與繪畫的交疊，回應觀看和影像之間逐漸趨於扁平的事實，他們認為，意識到細微的不合理之處才是重回現實的唯一途徑。

Read in English

Yi-Chia Huang and PUWEI Wu draw inspiration from everyday life and observations of visual culture imagery. In their past exhibitions, they have employed a keen perspective and guerrilla tactics, intervening in exhibition spaces with site-specific spray-painted works that engage in a dialogue with the flat layers and visual overload of contemporary societal imagery.

In this exhibition, they use spray paint as a creative medium, allowing their works to proliferate throughout various corners of the contemporary gallery. Some pieces are embedded along the viewer's path, like projected images, disrupting the everyday perceptions captured by the peripheral vision of the audience. The artwork "vision 404" at the entrance of the exhibition is an installation that combines sound and visuals, enticing the viewer's audiovisual experience. As the spray-painted images scatter along the exhibition path, the artists intend to create an indexical layout in the gallery. Only by completing the circuit around the exhibition can the audience piece together the appearance of the artwork. In an era saturated with flat images, Yi-Chia Huang and PUWEI Wu, through the intersection of images and painting, respond to the gradual flattening of the relationship between viewing and imagery. They believe that recognizing subtle inconsistencies is the only path back to reality.
