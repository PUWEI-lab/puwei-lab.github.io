大決闘

BIG DUEL

系列介紹 / Statement

《大決闘》是一個風格迥異的實驗系列，我搜集了各種特攝片的劇照，並著手研究特攝片的後製技術。特攝片出現在影像後製技術還未全面數位化的時代，當時的電影特效技術主要是利用底片曝光的化學反應製造出非現實的視覺效果。而日本特攝片靈活調配了鏡頭、搭景與微縮模型以及著怪獸皮套的演員，去製造怪獸比例上的強迫透視，再加入後製技術結合與怪獸互動的人類角色。這樣的拍攝手法在技術相對落後的時代其實非常先進，而且具有標誌性。我統整完各種電影技術後開始將他們作為繪畫圖層分佈的依據，用不同媒材的材料學特性安排畫面上色流程，拿簡單的劇照為素材，實踐各種以繪畫媒材回應特效的繪畫技術。

BIG DUEL is an experimental series marked by contrasting styles. I collected stills from tokusatsu films and began researching their post-production techniques. These films emerged before post-production had become fully digital, when cinematic effects relied largely on the chemical reactions of exposed film to create unreal images. Japanese tokusatsu combined camera work, sets, miniature models, and performers in monster suits to construct forced perspectives of scale, then used post-production to integrate human characters interacting with the monsters. These methods were both innovative and distinctive in an era of comparatively limited technology. After studying these techniques, I used them as the basis for arranging layers within my paintings. The material properties of different media determined the painting sequence, allowing me to use simple film stills to explore painterly responses to special effects.
