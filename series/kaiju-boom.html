怪獸繁榮

Kaiju Boom

系列介紹 / Statement

在哪跌倒就在哪爬起，新的系列被我命名作《怪獸繁榮》（Kaiju Boom, 2023-2026）。我將目光放在架上畫的操作可能，同時使用輕薄的噴塗技法搭配厚重的顏料堆疊效果，以紙膠帶割出銳利的造型，也用類似「扁平幽靈」的畫法在畫布上製造模糊的殘影。作品的圖像圍繞著特攝片的幾個概念：片場側拍、皮套戲服、微縮模型、強迫透視、核試爆、戰爭、文化商品、SOFUBI（軟膠玩具），用焦距不一的圖像拼湊，顯示出特攝片中時常帶有割裂相異的後製效果，回應片廠的結構、電影美術以及特效製作。

I named the new series Kaiju Boom (2023–2026). Focusing on the possibilities of easel painting, I combine thin layers of sprayed paint with thick accumulations of pigment, cut sharp shapes with masking tape, and use techniques resembling those of Flat Ghosts to produce blurred afterimages on canvas. The imagery revolves around several aspects of tokusatsu: behind-the-scenes photographs, monster suits, miniature models, forced perspective, nuclear tests, war, cultural merchandise, and SOFUBI (soft vinyl toys). By assembling images with different points of focus, the paintings reveal the fragmented and contrasting post-production effects often found in tokusatsu, responding to the structure of the studio, film art direction, and special-effects production.
